Inference contract
------------------
Input is BGRA, top-left, non-mirrored source pixels. The bridge crops a fixed
or face-anchored 192:256 ROI without using face roll, converts BGRA to RGB,
normalizes with mean [123.675,116.28,103.53] and std [58.395,57.12,57.375],
and invokes NCHW [1,3,256,192]. Outputs are SimCC x [1,26,384] and y
[1,26,512], decoded at half-resolution and projected back to normalized
top-left image coordinates. Halpe26 index/name order is exposed by the C API.

Core ML conversion/provider was attempted but is not validated for this asset.
The Swift adapter therefore defaults to CPU ONNX Runtime; opting into Core ML
is explicit and fails closed if the provider/session cannot be created. There
is no silent BlazePose fallback.
